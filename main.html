Purpose and Vision of Developing the Software

The idea for developing this software came from my own experience as a student at the academy. I noticed that after completing practice questions on paper, we often had to wait for an additional review class to understand the correct answers and rationales. This not only delayed the learning process but also consumed valuable preparation time.

I therefore thought of creating a digital practice platform that could provide instant results, detailed rationales, and an exam-like testing experience, similar to features normally available in paid question-bank platforms.

By eliminating repeated waiting periods for question reviews, the system can potentially save students several weeks of preparation time, allowing them to progress faster and focus immediately on their weak areas.

The interface is designed to closely resemble the official examination environment, making practice more realistic, engaging, and motivating. In addition, live statistics, saved progress, performance tracking, and question-review features allow students to continuously monitor their improvement.

Compared with traditional paper-based practice, the software provides a faster, more interactive, organized, and self-directed method of preparation, encouraging students to practice more questions while receiving immediate learning feedback.
